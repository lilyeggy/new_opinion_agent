## Executive summary

OpenAI announced GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.4 Pro for ChatGPT, the API, and Codex, presenting the release as a frontier model optimized for professional work, agentic workflows, computer use, and long-context execution. Independent coverage largely repeated the capability and benchmark framing, while an emerging narrative described the release as a shift from AI that answers questions toward AI that performs work. A counter-narrative questioned whether benchmark gains would translate into reliable user experience and pointed to backlash following earlier GPT releases.
